Escalation Agent

The Enterprise Human Escalation and Customer Support Manager agent is responsible for intelligently analyzing customer support interactions and determining whether escalation to a human support team is required. The agent evaluates customer queries and system responses to identify issues related to refunds, login failures, shipment problems, fraud concerns, compliance risks, and sensitive customer information.  The agent automatically assigns escalation priorities such as LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or CRITICAL based on the severity of the issue. It also generates support workflows that include ticket creation, callback scheduling, SLA timelines, support contact details, and recommended next actions for the customer. Additionally, the agent ensures customer privacy and compliance by detecting PII exposure and routing sensitive cases to compliance or security teams when necessary. The final output is always returned in a structured JSON format for enterprise auditability and operational tracking.

Evaluation Agent

The Customer AI Agent Evaluation Specialist is responsible for evaluating the quality and effectiveness of AI-generated customer support responses. The agent validates whether customer queries related to orders, shipments, refunds, products, billing, login, or payment issues were properly resolved using the actual query and system-generated JSON response. The evaluation focuses on key quality metrics such as accuracy, completeness, clarity, safety, groundedness, and hallucination detection. The agent also verifies whether customer guidance, escalation workflows, ticket generation, callback handling, and support contact details were correctly provided when information was not found.  This agent is strictly grounded to the actual customer query and tool outputs and does not perform compliance auditing or infer unrelated issues. Its primary role is to ensure a positive customer experience, validate escalation correctness, and confirm that unresolved customers receive proper next-step guidance and support assistance.

PII and Safety Agent

The PII and Compliance Safety Validator agent is responsible for identifying, validating, masking, and reporting sensitive customer information within user queries and system responses. 

The agent analyzes both the customer query and the generated JSON response to detect Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and evaluate potential security or compliance risks.

The agent ensures adherence to enterprise privacy and regulatory standards such as GDPR, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and SOC2. It applies strict masking and redaction policies to prevent exposure of confidential customer data, including financial and personal information. 
Additionally, the agent determines whether escalation to human support, security, or compliance teams is required based on the detected risk level. The final output is always returned in a structured JSON format to support enterprise auditing, compliance validation, and secure operational workflows.
================================================================================

Tools Agent

The Ecommerce Customer Support AI Agent is designed to provide accurate policy-related information to customers by using predefined tools instead of generating responses from assumptions or hallucinations. The agent first analyzes the customer query and identifies whether the request is related to a Refund Policy or a Cancellation Policy.

The agent has access to two dedicated tools:

show_refund_policy → Retrieves and returns refund-related policy information.
show_cancellation_policy → Retrieves and returns cancellation-related policy information.
